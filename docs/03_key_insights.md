# Key Insights

### 1. Subscription Length as the Strongest Churn Driver
Customers with longer subscription lengths showed significantly higher churn rates, when compared to short-term subscribers.<br>
**Interpretation:** The last months of the subscription lifecycle represent the highest-risk period, making later retention initiatives critical for reducing overall churn.

### 2. Engagement Rate is strongly linked to Churn
Customers with lower engagement rates showed higher churn rates than those with high engagement rates.<br>
**Interpretation:** Engagement rate is a key determiner of retention. Improving this can have a direct impact on reducing customer churn.

### 3. Unexpected Churn Pattern among Customers with Highest Engagement Rate
Customers with satisfaction scores of 9 and above have displayed higher-than-expected churn levels, despite the previously existing trend.<br>
**Interpretation:** Engagement rate alone may not fully explain retention behavior. Additional factors might be influencing churn decisions.

### 4. Asia Represents the Highest-Churn Region
Among all analyzed regions, Asia exhibited the highest concentration of churned customers.<br>
**Interpretation:** Market-specific strategies may be required to improve retention performance within this region.

### 5. South America Demonstrates Retention Instability
The distribution between retained and churned customers in South America was nearly balanced, with churn and retention occurring at similar levels. Subsequently, this region retained the highest number of customers, while having lowest churn rate.<br>
**Interpretation:** Market strategies of this region must be referred to, while formulating new strategies to improve retention of other regions.

### 6. Standard Plan Subscribers Exhibit the Highest Retention
Customers subscribed to the Standard plan demonstrated higher retention rates than those on Basic or Premium plans.<br>
**Interpretation:** The value proposition of the Standard plan is stronger than other subscription tiers. Customers may find more value than switch services or leaving.

### 7. Delayed Payment Behavior Is Associated With Elevated Churn
Customers with delayed payment histories displayed higher churn rates compared to customers who consistently paid on time.<br>
**Interpretation:** Payment behavior can serve as an early warning signal for churn. Delayed payments may indicate customer dissatisfaction, financial constraints, or reduced commitment to the platform.

### 8. Documentary Viewers Represent the Highest-Risk Content Segment
Customers who prefer 'Documentaries' exhibited the highest average churn risk scores among all genre groups.<br>
**Interpretation:** The documentary audience may have unmet content expectations or lower engagement levels. Additional content investments or improvements may be required to improve retention in this segment.

### 9. Device Usage Significantly Influences Engagement Patterns
Laptop users recorded the highest average daily watch hours, while Smart TV users recorded the lowest average watch hours.<br>
**Interpretation:** Viewing patterns vary significantly across devices. Platform usability, content discovery, and convenience may influence engagement behavior across different devices.
